# Veyra — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 8 November 2026  
**Service:** Veyra, a Discord bot operated by YukiShinobi  
**Discord application ID:** `1554091499719761961`

This policy describes personal data Veyra may collect or process when you interact with the bot or when it is installed in a Discord server. Exact data processed depends on commands, enabled features, Discord permissions and deployment settings.

## 1. Data Veyra may process

- **Discord identifiers and metadata:** user IDs, server/guild IDs, channel IDs, message IDs, roles, permissions, usernames and other information provided through Discord's API.
- **Command and conversation content:** slash-command inputs, messages addressed to Veyra or otherwise made available to its enabled features, prompts, responses and interaction context.
- **Server configuration and operational records:** server structure, roles, channels, onboarding settings, server plans, snapshots, saved memory, audit or operation logs, approved-guild settings and relevant moderation/automation metadata.
- **Simply Management data:** order descriptions, community requirements, selected packages, contact details, progress, previews and completion reports submitted or generated during fulfillment.
- **Voice or audio content:** if voice or text-to-speech capabilities are enabled and used, audio content or derived text may be processed as necessary to deliver the feature. Whether recordings are stored depends on implementation; do not assume audio is ephemeral.
- **Technical/security information:** error messages, service activity, timestamps and operational diagnostics needed to run and protect the bot.

Veyra does not need your Discord account password. Do not send passwords, payment-card information, or highly sensitive personal information to the bot.

## 2. Why data is processed

Data is processed to respond to commands and messages; provide AI assistance; execute authorized server-management workflows; maintain settings, memory and approved-guild records; process orders; detect abuse; troubleshoot errors; and protect reliability and security. Depending on the activity and applicable law, relevant lawful bases may include performance of a requested service, legitimate interests in operating and securing Veyra, consent where required, and legal obligations.

## 3. AI and external service providers

Veyra uses Discord infrastructure and may use hosting, database, logging, AI-model, speech or text-to-speech providers, depending on deployment configuration. Content submitted to AI/voice functions **may be transmitted to external providers** for processing. Their locations, data handling and retention depend on the actual services configured. This policy does not claim that user content is never shared with providers or never used for model training; those matters must be verified against the active provider settings and agreements.

Veyra does **not** claim to sell personal data. This is not a claim that all processing is confined to one country.

## 4. Data storage and retention

Some information, including approved-guild records, server memory, snapshots, orders and audit data, may be stored persistently in databases or operational storage. Logs and provider-side copies may also exist. **No universal fixed retention period is currently represented by this policy.** Data should be retained only as reasonably necessary for functionality, troubleshooting, security, legal obligations or dispute resolution. The operator should implement and document retention/deletion schedules as the deployment matures.

Removing the bot from a server does not necessarily delete historical records automatically. You may request review or deletion of stored information using the contact method below, subject to legal and technical exceptions.

## 5. Your controls and rights

Server owners and administrators can limit Veyra's access using Discord permissions, channel settings, feature configuration, or by removing the bot. You may be entitled, depending on applicable law, to request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or objection to certain processing, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To make a privacy request, contact **YukiShinobi** via the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/YukiShinobi) or an official Veyra support/contact channel operated on Discord. Include relevant Discord user/server IDs and the nature of the request, but **do not publish private personal data in a public GitHub issue**. Identity or authorization may need to be verified before a request can be completed.

UK users may also have the right to complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

## 6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect data, such as access controls and controlled credentials. No online system is guaranteed to be fully secure. Report suspected misuse or a security incident privately through the contact route above rather than publishing sensitive details.

## 7. Children

Veyra is intended for users permitted to use Discord under Discord's minimum-age rules and applicable law. Do not use Veyra to solicit personal information from children.

## 8. Changes and contact

This policy may be revised when Veyra's features, data practices, providers or retention procedures change. The effective date will be updated as appropriate.
